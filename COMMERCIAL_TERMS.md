# Paid Beta Terms

Effective date: 11 August 2026

These terms apply to the paid document-reliability beta offered by Wessel ter Laak through this
repository and its associated website. Live self-service checkout remains disabled until the
seller's business registration details are published.

## Business customers only

The paid beta is offered only to businesses and professionals acting for business purposes. It is
not a consumer service. Prices are shown excluding VAT; applicable VAT will be added to the invoice
or checkout where required.

## Forming an order

An email request is not an accepted order. An order starts only after the seller confirms that the
documents are in scope, confirms the price and delivery target, provides these terms, and receives
payment. The applicable offer JSON records the agreed base scope.

Do not attach documents to the initial email. The initial request should contain only a description
of the document type, approximate page count, desired output, and whether personal data is present.

## Scope and delivery

The Profile Feasibility Check covers one born-digital PDF or DOCX of up to 10 pages and 5 detected
tables. The Document Reliability Pilot covers one recurring layout, up to 5 similar documents, and
up to 50 pages in total. It delivers a versioned profile, a checked expected mapping for the accepted
sample set, and an evaluation report. Delivery targets begin after all files are accepted as in scope
and payment is confirmed.

Scanned or image-only documents, handwriting, encrypted files, charts, image extraction, and manual
data entry are excluded unless separately agreed. The beta does not accept patient data or other
special-category personal data.

## Review and corrections

Outputs are manually checked against extraction warnings and the agreed expected mapping, but
automated table extraction can still misread complex or previously unseen layouts. An `accepted`
decision means the configured profile checks passed; it is not a guarantee that the source document
is truthful or that unrepresented layouts will perform at the measured rate. The customer remains
responsible for approving output before quality, financial, regulatory, medical, or other
consequential use.

One correction round is included when requested within 7 calendar days after delivery and remains
within the agreed source documents and output format.

## Refunds and cancellation

If an accepted Profile Feasibility Check produces no usable structured table, the customer may choose
a full refund or cancel before any separately quoted manual rework. If the Pilot cannot produce a
usable versioned profile and evaluation baseline, the customer may choose a full refund or approve a
revised scope before additional work.

Either party may cancel before payment. After work starts, refunds for reasons outside the failure
conditions above are handled according to the completed portion of the agreed work.

## Customer responsibilities

The customer confirms that it has the right to share the documents and request their processing. The
customer must redact personal, patient, confidential, or regulated data that is not necessary for the
pilot. Passwords, credentials, and payment details must never be sent with documents.
