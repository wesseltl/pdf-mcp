Fulfillment

A controlled four-step handoff.

  1. DescribeEmail the document type, page count, and desired output. No attachment.
  2. ConfirmScope, secure transfer, payment, and delivery timing are confirmed.
  3. ConvertThe tables are extracted, exported, and manually checked for warnings.
  4. ValidateYou receive the files, review notes, and one correction round.

Good fit

Structured tables in digital business documents.

  • Lab reports and certificates of analysis
  • Invoices, statements, and operational reports
  • Repeated layouts that need Excel, CSV, or JSON
  • Workflows where uncertainty must be surfaced for review

Out of scope

No OCR or sensitive health data.

  • Scanned or image-only PDFs
  • Handwriting, charts, images, and password-protected files
  • Patient data or other special-category personal data
  • Manual data entry when no usable table is detected
  • Production hosting or ongoing integrations in the fixed-price offers

The separate free hosted beta is proprietary on the server side and measures bounded operational usage. The paid service remains available for a manually reviewed result on a fixed document set.
