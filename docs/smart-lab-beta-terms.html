LabOverlay Beta Terms

Effective 11 August 2026

These terms apply to the free, invitation-based LabOverlay desktop beta operated by Wessel ter Laak. The beta is an evaluation release, not a production laboratory system.

Beta access

Access is request-only and may be limited, changed, suspended, or ended during the beta. An application does not guarantee an invitation date, continued access, support response, or production service level.

Appropriate evaluation data

Start with synthetic, non-sensitive, or explicitly approved internal sample data. Do not attach laboratory files or document contents to the access-request email.

Do not use the beta for patient data, special-category personal data, credentials, payment-card data, malware, hostile documents, or information whose handling requires a production processing agreement. The current beta has no OCR and is not validated for regulated quality release, clinical decisions, or safety-critical decisions.

Local processing

LabOverlay reads the selected source folder without modifying its files. Its browser interface binds to loopback, runtime assets are bundled, and no-egress mode disables external providers and telemetry. The local index is stored in owner-restricted SQLite files. Index content is not application-encrypted, so use encrypted storage and an appropriate operating-system account where required.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for authorization to index the selected files, workstation security, backups, reviewing source evidence, and independently validating results before use. A detected or undetected issue is not a substitute for laboratory, quality, regulatory, or safety review.

Feedback and contact information

Beta request emails and voluntary feedback may contain the contact and use-case details supplied by the requester. Do not include source records or confidential document contents. Feedback may be used to improve the product and prioritize modules.

Availability and liability

The beta is provided as-is for evaluation. Indexing may be incomplete or incorrect, and releases may change storage or behavior.
